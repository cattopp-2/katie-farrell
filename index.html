Getting started

Check in with your system
First thing
At the start of every session, Claude reads your context files. Confirm it's loaded correctly before you start work.
Say: Who am I and what am I working on?
Write any content
Most used
The content router takes any idea — a topic, a theme, a story you want to tell — and asks which format you want. It handles everything from there.
Type: /content-router
Example prompt for you "I want to write about the moment I realised my exhaustion was my body's way of communicating — not something to push through"
End every session with wrap-up
Daily habit
Run this every time before you close Claude. It saves your open threads, captures any feedback, and makes sure nothing gets lost between sessions. Without this, the system doesn't compound.
Type: /wrap-up
